Origin Story

BEFORE THEY BECAME SONGS,
THEY WERE WORDS.

"30 कविताएँ जो पहले पन्नों पर रची गईं, अब संगीत के सुरों में ढल चुकी हैं।"

Humsafar began as an intimate printed volume of classical Hindi poetry by Pooja Sawariya. Thirty selected poems from these printed pages were chosen to undergo a second transformation—reimagined with studio orchestration, melody, and vocal arrangement. On 20 August 2026, this digital index will unlock its full musical layer.
